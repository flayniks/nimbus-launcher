IT GOES IN GAME

Nimbus Core ships inside the launcher and rides along in every Fabric and Quilt game.

  • An animated loading screen instead of Mojang's red one. Spin, bounce, splash, pulse or flip.
  • Nimbus Features: 18 HUD boxes you drag anywhere, zoom, fullbright, FPS presets and 20+ tweaks.
  • Skins & Capes right from the title screen and the pause menu.
  • Your own menu background: a picture, the Nimbus glow, or the classic panorama.
